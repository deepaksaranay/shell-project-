The throat history is hidden in the thrust record. At fixed chamber pressure, thrust depends on throat area both directly and through the nozzle area ratio. Inverting the thrust coefficient sample by sample gives $ A_t(t) $ once the jet efficiency is known, and the efficiency can be calibrated in the early burn, before the throat starts to erode. Linear or pressure-weighted interpolation between the pre- and post-fire throat diameters misses the erosion onset delay, and that biases both the throat history and the burn-rate exponent. With $ A_t(t) $ known, the overall mass balance gives $ c^* $. Web regression through the BATES geometry then gives $ r(p) $.
